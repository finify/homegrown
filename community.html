Join the fruit and veg groupHire us

Weekly fruit and vegetables group

Learn to grow your own food alongside our team. The produce is shared with volunteers and some is donated to Sunderland Soup Kitchen.

What you'll learn

  • Propagation: growing new plants from seed and cuttings
  • Succession planting, for a harvest all season
  • Pest control without harsh chemicals
  • Feeding and watering
  • Pruning
