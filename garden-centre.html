The garden centre

Bedding, hanging baskets and perennials, most of them grown here in Thompson Park. Open seven days a week.

Get directionsTrade enquiries

Grown here

What's on the benches changes through the year. [CONFIRM: current stock]

  • Bedding plants

    Grown from plugs in our own polytunnels, in trays and pots.

  • Hanging baskets

    Planted up on site. Petunias, trailing lobelia and more.

  • Perennials

    Salvias, scabious, daisies and cottage-garden favourites.

  • Spring colour

    Primroses and polyanthus in every shade.

Every plant you buy helps fund training and work experience for the people who grew it.
