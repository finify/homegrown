What we collect and why

Health and support information

Our referral form lets you tell us, if you choose, about support someone needs. This is special category data under UK GDPR. We only use it to plan support, and only with your explicit consent. [CONFIRM: retention period and who can see it]

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent at any time. Contact us, or complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

Accessibility

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Text is set in Atkinson Hyperlegible, designed for readers with low vision. Every page works with a keyboard and a screen reader, colours meet contrast guidelines, and moving effects switch off if your device asks for reduced motion. Our day opportunities page has an Easy Read version.
