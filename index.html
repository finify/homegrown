Growing skills and community

Confidence, training and real work for young people and adults, at our garden centre and nursery in Thompson Park, Sunderland.

Day opportunitiesVisit the garden centre

We grow plants and people on a former council yard in Thompson Park.

Who are you here for?

Choose the route that fits. Each one has the details you need and a way to get in touch.

  • For individuals and familiesSupported days of gardening, training and work experience. See what a day is like.
  • For professionals and referrersWho it suits, funding routes, and how to make a referral.
  • For gardeners and visitorsBedding, baskets and perennials, grown here. Open seven days.

What a day here looks like

Real work with real plants, alongside staff who know you. Every day is a little different, depending on the season.

Swipe to see more

  • Potting and propagating

    Sowing seeds, taking cuttings and potting on in the nursery and polytunnels.

  • Growing food

    Raised beds of onions, squash and greens. The harvest is shared and some goes to Sunderland Soup Kitchen.

  • Working in the shop

    Real retail work experience in the garden centre: watering, displays, helping customers.

  • Learning together

    Workshops, joinery and beekeeping with a horticultural trainer and health mentors alongside.

  • Looking after the bees

    Suited up, checking the hives and learning how a colony works.

How to join, in four steps

No forms to fill in on the first day and nothing to decide alone. Each step is a conversation.

An illustration of a seed in a plant pot growing into a plant with three leaves as you move through the steps.

  1. Step 1 of 4

    Get in touch

    Fill in the form, phone us or email. You can do this yourself, or a family member, carer or social worker can do it for you.

    We reply within[CONFIRM: 2 working days]

  2. Step 2 of 4

    Come and see us

    Visit Thompson Park, meet the team and try a taster session. Bring someone with you if that helps.

    [CONFIRM: taster session offer]

  3. Step 3 of 4

    Agree your days

    Together we choose the days, the activities and the support that suit you. We talk about funding with you or your referrer.

    [CONFIRM: funding routes]

  4. Step 4 of 4

    Start growing

    Your first day. A member of staff meets you, shows you round and works alongside you.
