Over £468K to help young people thrive

A Youth Investment Fund grant of over £468K will create exciting new initiatives to help young people thrive.
Sunderland Home Grown is a rich environment to help young people and adults with learning disabilities and mental health problems to thrive and enjoy life, by creating training and employment opportunities and promoting social and therapeutic horticulture.
Read our story on the Youth Investment Fund website
“Everyone who comes to Sunderland Home Grown has a unique contribution to make and no matter what they do, whether they are potting plants or working in the shop, they all play a significant role in growing our produce. Young people love being here and get real joy out of seeing that they have grown something and made it happen. It’s quite remarkable given that many young people who live in the city don’t know how vegetables are produced. And everyone who comes here always leaves with a big smile on their face.”
Gary Hillery
Manager and Director, Sunderland Home Grown CIC
Training and work placements

What we offer young people

We offer young people accredited and non-accredited horticulture training programmes and supported work placements, including:
  • Practice-based horticulture
  • Site maintenance
  • Producing plants and produce
  • Retail experience
  • Dealing with the public
Young people are supported by health mentors and a horticultural trainer. Over the years we have developed links with schools, colleges and academies, and can offer young people new life experiences as well as the joy of growing produce.

A workshop, glasshouse and training centre

We are constructing a vibrant community workshop, a spacious commercial glasshouse, and a classroom and training centre.
In our commitment to inclusivity, we’re taking additional measures to ensure accessibility for everyone. Our outdoor areas are being tarmacked, creating a smoother and more user-friendly experience, particularly for wheelchair users.

An off-grid, sustainable garden centre

The second part of the initiative is a new garden centre designed to be off-grid and sustainable, using fewer resources. Our storage containers will have solar panels, and there’s a huge water tank to collect rainwater.
Our new polytunnel, canopy and fencing have already been set up, creating a nurturing environment for our plants and visitors alike. The whole garden centre shop was assembled from a flat-pack kit, an efficient way of building that fits our sustainability goals.
“The news of securing a Youth Investment Fund grant is still sinking in. We’re such a small company and the difference it will make is mind-blowing. It means we can reach more young people, create more joy, and be there for future generations. Sunderland Home Grown is a place of mindfulness and wellness: it’s not just about learning new skills, it’s also a chance to come in and smell the roses.”
