# Youth Investment Fund page (/youth-investment-fund)

`before/youth-investment-fund.html` is the page as it was (your backup). `after/youth-investment-fund.html` is the new one.

## Install
1. Open the **Youth Investment Fund** page in Developer Mode, select all its HTML, delete it and paste `after/youth-investment-fund.html`.
2. Nothing to link: the styles are in `base.css`, already linked site-wide.
3. Preview desktop, tablet and phone, then **Publish**:
   `https://websitebuilder.123-reg.co.uk/site/0432dc74/youth-investment-fund?preview=true&insitepreview=true&dm_device=desktop`

## What's on the page
1. **Hero:** the YIF logo, "Over £468K to help young people thrive", the grant line (linking to your story on the Youth Investment Fund website), the paragraph about Home Grown, a button to that story, and the photo of a young man watering in the polytunnel.
2. **Gary's quote** on a deep green panel, with his photo, name and role.
3. **What we offer young people:** the training and work placement text, the five areas as ticked labels, mentors and links with schools.
4. **What the grant is building:** six icon cards (community workshop, commercial glasshouse, classroom and training centre, tarmacked paths for wheelchair users, off-grid garden centre with solar panels, rainwater tank). Then two text-and-photo sections: the workshop, glasshouse and training centre, and the off-grid garden centre.
5. **The second quote** on a gold panel, with the beekeeping and thumbs-up photos.
6. **Closing panel:** "Know a young person who could thrive here?" with **Get in touch** and **Call 0191 549 9444**.

Every block is a normal Duda text or image widget, so edit words and photos in the editor. The old page's words are kept, lightly tidied and split under headings; nothing new is claimed.
