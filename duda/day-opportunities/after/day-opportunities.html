For over 11 years, Sunderland Home Grown has provided meaningful work-based day opportunities for adults with disabilities and additional support needs. Based in the heart of Thompson Park, we help people develop practical skills, confidence, independence and friendships through real, purposeful activities in a welcoming community.

Independence

Meaningful work-based activities

Real, purposeful work in our nursery, workshop, gardens and garden centre.
Grow and care for plants and vegetables
Learn woodworking and construction
Explore arts and crafts with a qualified therapist
Care for bees and learn about their role
Gain hands-on nursery experience

Support that fits

We offer group and individual one-to-one support, with flexible attendance options to meet your needs. We are fully wheelchair accessible.

Tailored support plans and funding

You may be able to use a Direct Payment or Personal Budget to pay for our service. Ask your social worker or local council about your eligibility.
