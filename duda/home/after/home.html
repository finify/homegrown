We grow plants and people.

We’re a community interest company providing social and therapeutic horticulture and horticulture training for young people and adults with learning disabilities and mental health challenges.

We’ve turned a former council maintenance site in Thompson Park into a growing and training facility that’s open to everyone.

What a day here looks like

A relaxed and friendly place to work, with a variety of tasks through the day in the nursery, the vegetable gardens and the garden centre.

In the nursery

Potting, watering and caring for bedding and perennials in the polytunnels.

Growing food

Fruit and vegetables in the raised beds and polytunnels.

In the garden centre

Retail work experience, helping deliver our high standard of customer service.

With the bees
