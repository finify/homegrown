About us

Sunderland Home Grown is a community interest company. Since 2017 we have turned a former council maintenance site in Thompson Park into a nursery, garden centre and training site.
Come and see us

From bare ground to growing

In 2017 we took on a former council maintenance site in Thompson Park, Southwick, and started clearing it.

Support from Power to Change transformed the site. Today it has polytunnels, raised beds, a garden centre and a training base.

Our plants now grow at Durham Cathedral, Lumley Castle, Palace Green and Sherburn Village. More importantly, so do the people who raise them.

What we do

A garden centre and plant nursery
Retail and wholesale bedding, hanging baskets and perennials, grown on site.
Therapeutic horticulture
Training and work for young people and adults with learning disabilities and mental health challenges.
A community hub
Interest groups, workshops and community gardening in Southwick, Fulwell and the city centre.
