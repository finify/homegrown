About us

Sunderland Home Grown is a community interest company. Since 2017 we have turned a former council maintenance yard into a nursery, garden centre and training site.

Read our story

From bare ground to growing

In 2017 we took on a former council maintenance site in Thompson Park, Southwick. It was rubble and bare earth.

Support from Power to Change transformed the site. Today it holds polytunnels, raised beds, a garden centre and a training base.

Our plants now grow at Durham Cathedral, Lumley Castle, Palace Green and Sherburn Village. More importantly, so do the people who raise them.

What we do

  • A garden centre and plant nursery

    Retail and wholesale bedding, hanging baskets and perennials.

  • Therapeutic horticulture

    Training and work for young people and adults with learning disabilities and mental health challenges.

  • A community hub

    Interest groups, workshops and community gardening.
