What you can do here

Choose what you enjoy. Most people try a bit of everything, then find their favourite.

  • Nursery and propagation

    Sowing, pricking out, potting on and caring for thousands of bedding plants.

  • Vegetable gardens

    Raised beds and polytunnels of onions, squash, salad and greens.

  • Landscaping

    Planting schemes and floral displays, from the first sketch to the last plant.

  • Community gardening

    Out and about in Southwick and Fulwell, working with Friends of Thompson Park.

  • Retail work experience

    Serving customers, stock and displays in our garden centre.

  • Beekeeping

    Suiting up to look after our hives.
